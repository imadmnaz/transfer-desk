              Assurance
              
            
              Transfer Desk never marks a transfer ready when it should be blocked or sent to a lawyer.

                  What testing caught
                  
          
                    
            
                  • A recorded exercise of Helion's right of first refusal was ignored when the Transfer Notice was not evidenced, which could have cleared a void transfer. It now blocks, with four regression tests.
                    • 
            
                  • Incomplete facts made the engine stop with an error. It now sends them to a lawyer and names the missing fact, and a fuzz test covers every control value in every scenario.
