We refer to the Amended and Restated Agreement of Limited Partnership of Northgate Helion SPV LP (the **"Partnership"**) (the **"LPA"**). This letter is entered into under Section 12.4 of the LPA with Harbour Family Office LLC (**"Harbour"**) in connection with its subscription for an Interest. Capitalized terms not defined in this letter have the meanings given in the LPA.

**1. Harbour Transferees.** Notwithstanding Sections 8.1 and 8.2 of the LPA, Harbour may Transfer all or part of its Interest without the consent of the General Partner to (a) any Affiliate of Harbour, or (b) any fund or account managed or advised by Harbour Capital Advisers LLC (each, a **"Harbour Transferee"**), subject to the conditions in Section 8.4 of the LPA.

**2. Deemed Consent.** Where Harbour requests the General Partner's consent to any other Transfer, the General Partner shall respond within ten Business Days after receipt of a complete request. A request is complete if it identifies the proposed transferee, the part of the Interest to be Transferred, the price and the proposed completion date. If the General Partner does not respond within that period, its consent shall be deemed given at the end of that period.

**3. Personal Rights.** The rights in this letter are personal to Harbour. They do not pass to any transferee of Harbour's Interest and may not be relied on by any other Limited Partner.

**4. No Effect on Other Obligations.** Nothing in this letter affects (a) the conditions in Section 8.4 of the LPA, or (b) any obligation arising under the Portfolio Company Documents, which bind the Partnership and not the General Partner alone.

**5. Governing Law.** This letter is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
